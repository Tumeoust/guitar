Fretview

Play along on a real guitar. You see the guitar the way you see your own when you look down at it: headstock on the left, thickest string closest to you.

  • Chord lessons: learn one chord at a time. The microphone listens and tells you when it sounds right, or which string needs fixing.
  • Left hand: coloured dots on the fretboard show which finger goes where. The next shape fades in with a shrinking ring.
  • Right hand: arrows and dots slide towards the white line over the soundhole. Strum or pick when they reach it.

Space = play / pause · R = restart · ← / → = jump a bar · drag to move the camera
