Get ready

Fretview

Play along on a real guitar. Like Guitar Hero, the screen looks along the strings: the neck on top, following your fretting hand, and the strumming lane below. Player view in the settings shows the whole guitar the way you see your own.

  • Chord lessons: learn one chord at a time. The microphone listens and tells you when it sounds right, or which string needs fixing. Then practise changing between them: at your own pace, against the clock, and finally in time with a metronome.
  • Left hand: coloured dots on the fretboard show which finger goes where, in Guitar Hero colours: green, red, yellow, blue. The next shape fades in with a shrinking ring.
  • Right hand: arrows and dots come down the lane towards the white line over the soundhole. Strum or pick when they reach it.
