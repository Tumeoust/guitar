Get ready

Fretview

Play along on a real guitar, Guitar Hero style. Coloured dots on the neck show where your fingers go, and strums fall down to the white line.

  • Chord lessons: learn one chord at a time. The microphone listens and tells you when it sounds right, or which string needs fixing. Then practise changing between them: at your own pace, against the clock, and finally in time with a metronome.
  • Play songs: play along at full speed, or slower and the song waits until you get each chord right.

How to read the screen

The camera looks up the neck from the body, like the note highway in Guitar Hero. The nut is at the top and the strings run E A D G B e from left to right, the way chord charts draw them.

Left hand: where the fingers go
index finger
middle finger
ring finger
pinky
○at the nut: play the string open
✕at the nut: don't play the string
hollow ring: where that finger goes next
The colours are the fret buttons of Guitar Hero 3: green, red, yellow, blue. A filled dot sits just behind the fret wire, where the fingertip presses. A ring round a filled dot means that finger stays put for the next chord. Once the last strum of a chord has gone by, the next chord fills in, so you can move early.
Right hand: when to strum or pick
↓strum down, thick → thin strings
↑strum up, thin → thick strings
●pick that string (finger colour, white = open)
Strums and picks fall down the lane below your hand. A strum is a bar across the strings to hit. Strum or pick when it reaches the white line. The first strum of each new chord has the chord's name beside it, so you can see the change coming.
Keys
Space = play / pause · Esc = pause / back · R = restart · ← / → = jump a bar · M = magnify the neck · drag to move the camera (Player and Top view)
